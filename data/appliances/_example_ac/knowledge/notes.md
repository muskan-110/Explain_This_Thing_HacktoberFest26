# GENERIC SAMPLE - Split AC Remote Control Manual Notes

## Power Button
Turns the air conditioner ON or OFF. Press once to start cooling or heating, press again to switch off the appliance completely.

## Fan speed
Controls how strongly the air blows. Press repeatedly to cycle between Low, Medium, High, and Auto fan modes.

## Timer
Sets automatic ON or OFF timers for the air conditioner. Press to set the hours after which the AC turns on or off automatically.

## Mode Selection
Switches between operation modes: Cool (for cold air), Heat (for warm air), Dry (dehumidifier mode), and Fan Only (circulates air without cooling).

## Temperature Adjustment
Use the Up and Down arrows to adjust the desired room temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).

## Eco Mode / Energy Saver
Activates energy-saving operation to reduce electricity consumption while maintaining comfortable room temperature.
