# AC Remote Manual Notes

## Power (round green button)
The round green button with the power symbol. It turns the AC on or off. How to use: point the remote at the AC and press it once to switch on, press it again to switch off.

## Temperature Up / Down arrows (long green bar)
The long green bar with an up arrow and a down arrow. It sets how cold or warm the room gets. How to use: press the down arrow to make the room cooler, the up arrow to make it warmer. The screen shows the new number.

## MODE
MODE button. It chooses how the AC works: Auto, Cool (snowflake picture), Dry (drop picture), Fan only, or Heat (sun picture). How to use: press MODE again and again until the snowflake shows on the screen for cooling.

## FAN
FAN button. It changes how strongly the air blows: Auto, Low, Medium or High. How to use: press FAN again and again until the speed you like shows. More bars on the screen means a stronger fan.

## SLEEP
SLEEP button. It slowly adjusts the temperature during the night so you stay comfortable and it saves electricity. It switches the AC off by itself after some hours. How to use: press SLEEP once at bedtime. Press it again to cancel.

## TURBO
TURBO button. It runs the AC at full power for a short time to cool or warm the room very fast. How to use: press TURBO when the room is very hot, then press it again to go back to normal. It uses more electricity.

## AUTO CLEAN
AUTO CLEAN button. It runs a self-cleaning cycle that dries and cleans the inside of the indoor unit. The AC does not cool the room during this cycle. How to use: press AUTO CLEAN once and wait until it finishes. The button glows green while it is on.

## LED DISPLAY
LED DISPLAY button. It switches the small light and display on the front of the indoor unit on or off. It does not switch the AC off. How to use: press it once at night to turn the light off, press again to turn it back on.

## SWING (two buttons)
There are two SWING buttons. The one with left-right arrows moves the air flap sideways. The one with up-down arrows moves the flap up and down. How to use: press a SWING button once to start moving, press it again to stop and keep the air pointing where it is.

## TIMER
TIMER button. It makes the AC switch on or off by itself after a set time. How to use: press TIMER, set the time with HR and MIN, then press TIMER again to confirm. Exact steps can differ a little between remotes.

## CLOCK
CLOCK button. It sets the time shown on the remote screen. How to use: press CLOCK, set the time with HR and MIN, then press CLOCK again to save.

## HR and MIN (hour and minute)
HR changes the hours and MIN changes the minutes. They work together with CLOCK and TIMER. How to use: press CLOCK or TIMER first, then press HR and MIN to change the numbers.

## Screen (temperature, mode picture and time)
The screen is not a button. It shows the set temperature (for example 16 degrees), a picture of the mode (a snowflake means cooling), the fan speed bars and the clock. Nothing to press here.
