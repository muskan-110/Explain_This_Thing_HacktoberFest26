# Dishwasher Manual Notes

## POWER and START / PAUSE
The big round button on the left has POWER written above it and START/PAUSE below it. It switches the machine on, starts the chosen wash, and pauses it. How to use: press once to switch on, choose a program, then press it to start. Press it during a wash to pause, and again to continue.

## NORMAL
NORMAL program button. It is the everyday wash for normally dirty dishes. How to use: press NORMAL until its light comes on, then press START/PAUSE to begin.

## HEAVY
HEAVY program button (pot picture). It is a stronger, longer wash for very dirty pots, pans and dishes with stuck-on food. How to use: press HEAVY until its light comes on, then press START/PAUSE.

## LIGHT
Two program buttons are marked LIGHT. The one with the wine glass picture is a gentle wash for glasses and lightly dirty dishes. The one with wavy lines is another short, light wash. How to use: press the LIGHT program you want, then press START/PAUSE.

## RINSE
RINSE button. It gives the dishes a quick rinse with no soap. Use it for dishes you will wash later, so food does not dry on them. How to use: press RINSE, then press START/PAUSE.

## DRY and sun picture button
The DRY button (drop picture) and the button with the sun picture are about drying. They make the dishes finish warm and dry. How to use: press once to turn drying on, press again to turn it off, before you press START/PAUSE.

## Display (00:30)
The blue screen is not a button. It shows the time, usually how long the wash will take or how much is left. Nothing to press here.
