# Microwave Manual Notes

## Start
START button (bottom right). It begins cooking after you have set the time or chosen a menu. How to use: put the food in, close the door, set the time or menu, then press START. Never start the microwave when it is empty.

## Stop / Reset
STOP/RESET button (bottom left). Press once to pause or stop cooking. Press a second time to clear everything you set. How to use: press once to pause, press twice to cancel completely.

## Micro Power
MICRO POWER button. It chooses how strong the microwave is. High power cooks fast, low power heats gently or melts. How to use: press MICRO POWER again and again until the level you want shows, set the time, then press START.

## Grill
GRILL button. It browns and crisps the top of food using the grill heater instead of microwaves. Good for toast and grilled snacks. How to use: put the food on the grill rack, press GRILL, set the time, then press START.

## Convection
CONVECTION button (top row). It works like an oven with hot air, for baking cakes and roasting. How to use: press CONVECTION again and again to choose the temperature, set the time, then press START.

## Combination
COMBINATION button. It uses the microwave together with grill or convection heat, so food cooks faster and also browns. How to use: press COMBINATION, set the time, then press START. Check the cooking guide for the dish.

## Defrost (frozen food)
AUTO DEFROST button. It thaws frozen food by weight. How to use: put the frozen food in, press AUTO DEFROST, use the UP and DOWN buttons to set the weight, then press START.

## Timer and Clock
TIMER/CLOCK button. It sets the clock, or works as a kitchen timer that counts down without cooking. How to use: press TIMER/CLOCK, enter the time with the number or time buttons, then press START. Steps can differ slightly by model.

## 10 min, 1 min and 10 sec (cooking time buttons)
These three buttons add cooking time. Each press adds 10 minutes, 1 minute or 10 seconds. How to use: press them until the time you want shows, for example 1 min pressed twice is 2 minutes, then press START.

## Menu and Weight (101 Auto Menu)
The 101 AUTO MENU / WEIGHT area works with the UP and DOWN buttons. They choose the menu number or the weight of the food. How to use: press a food button such as REHEAT, then press UP or DOWN to set the weight, then press START.

## Up and Down arrows
UP and DOWN buttons. They change the number on the screen: the weight of the food or the menu number. How to use: press UP to raise the number, DOWN to lower it, then press START.

## Reheat
REHEAT button. It automatically reheats food that is already cooked, such as a plate of food or a cup of milk. How to use: put the food in, press REHEAT, set the amount with UP or DOWN if needed, then press START.

## Main Course
MAIN DISH button. It cooks main dishes like rice or curry automatically. How to use: press MAIN DISH, use UP or DOWN to set the weight, then press START.

## Side (Gravy) and Side (Dry)
There are two SIDE DISH buttons. SIDE DISH (GRAVY) is for vegetables or curries with gravy. SIDE DISH (DRY) is for dry vegetable dishes. How to use: press the one that matches your food, set the weight with UP or DOWN, then press START.

## Soup and Snack
SOUP/SNACK button. It heats soups or cooks snacks automatically. How to use: press SOUP/SNACK, use UP or DOWN to set the amount, then press START.

## Tandoori
TANDOORI/GRILL button. It cooks tandoori-style food like tikka or kebabs using the grill. How to use: put the food on the grill rack, press TANDOORI/GRILL, set the weight with UP or DOWN, then press START.

## Japanese
JAPANESE button. It is an automatic menu for Japanese-style dishes. How to use: press JAPANESE, choose the dish number with UP or DOWN as shown in the cooking guide, then press START.

## Dessert
DESSERT button. It is an automatic menu for desserts. How to use: press DESSERT, choose the menu number or weight with UP or DOWN, then press START.

## Cake and Cookie
CAKE/COOKIE button. It is an automatic menu for baking cakes and cookies in oven mode. How to use: press CAKE/COOKIE, choose the menu with UP or DOWN, then press START. Follow the cooking guide for the dish and tray.
