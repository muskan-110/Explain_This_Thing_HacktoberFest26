# TV Remote Manual Notes

## Power (red button)
The red button at the top left with the power symbol. It turns the TV on or off. How to use: press it once to switch the TV on, press again to switch it off.

## SOURCE
SOURCE button. It chooses what the TV shows: TV channels, set-top box, game console, USB or HDMI. How to use: press SOURCE, move with the arrow buttons to the one you want, then press the center button.

## Number buttons 0 to 9
The number buttons type in a channel number. How to use: press the digits of the channel, for example 5 then 2 for channel 52, and wait a moment for the TV to switch.

## TTX / MIX (Teletext)
TTX/MIX button. It shows teletext pages, which are text pages sent with some TV channels. Many channels do not have it. How to use: press TTX/MIX once to open teletext, press again to close it.

## PRE-CH (previous channel)
PRE-CH button. It goes back to the channel you watched before this one. How to use: press PRE-CH once to jump back, press again to jump forward.

## Volume (VOL + and VOL -)
The VOL button with plus and minus. It changes how loud the TV is. How to use: press + to make it louder, press - to make it quieter.

## MUTE
MUTE button. It switches the sound off without changing the volume. How to use: press MUTE once to silence the TV, press again to bring the sound back.

## CH (channel up and down)
The CH button with up and down arrows. It changes the TV channel one step at a time. How to use: press the up side for the next channel, the down side for the previous channel.

## CH LIST (channel list)
CH LIST button. It shows a list of all channels on the screen. How to use: press CH LIST, move with the arrow buttons to a channel, then press the center button.

## Home (house button)
The button with the house picture. It opens the Home screen with apps and settings. How to use: press it once to open the Home screen. Press RETURN or EXIT to leave.

## NETFLIX
NETFLIX button. It opens the Netflix app directly. It needs an internet connection and a Netflix account. How to use: press NETFLIX once.

## PRIME VIDEO
PRIME VIDEO button. It opens the Amazon Prime Video app directly. It needs an internet connection and an account. How to use: press PRIME VIDEO once.

## Samsung TV Plus
Samsung TV Plus button. It opens Samsung's free TV channels, which need an internet connection. How to use: press it once, then use the arrow buttons to pick a channel.

## Arrow buttons (up, down, left, right)
The four triangle arrows around the center button. They move the highlight on the screen up, down, left and right. How to use: press an arrow to move, then press the center button to choose.

## Center button (Enter / OK / select)
The button in the middle of the arrows, with the box-and-arrow picture. It confirms what is highlighted on the screen. How to use: move to something with the arrows, then press this button to open or select it.

## GUIDE
GUIDE button. It shows the TV program guide, a list of what is on now and next. It may be empty if the TV is connected through a set-top box. How to use: press GUIDE, move with the arrows, press GUIDE or EXIT to close.

## RETURN
RETURN button. It goes back one step, to the previous screen or menu. How to use: press RETURN once for each step back.

## EXIT
EXIT button. It closes the menu or app and goes back to normal TV. How to use: press EXIT once.

## Colored buttons A B C D
The red A, green B, yellow C and blue D buttons. They are shortcuts used by some menus and teletext pages. The screen shows what each color does. How to use: press the color shown on the screen. They do nothing in normal TV watching.

## SETTINGS
SETTINGS button. It opens the quick settings menu for picture, sound and other options. How to use: press SETTINGS, move with the arrows, press the center button to choose. Press RETURN to go back.

## INFO
INFO button. It shows details about the channel or program you are watching. How to use: press INFO once. Press it again or EXIT to hide it.

## AD / SUBT. (audio description and subtitles)
AD/SUBT. button. It switches audio description or subtitles on the screen on or off, if the program offers them. How to use: press AD/SUBT. once, and pick the option shown on the screen.

## Playback buttons (rewind, play, pause, forward, stop)
The row with rewind, play, pause and fast forward, and the stop square above. They control a video that is playing, for example from USB or an app. How to use: press play to watch, pause to stop for a moment, rewind or forward to move through the video. They do nothing on normal live TV channels.
